# ElectroSchem 1.3.0rc16

## Cable routing

- Moving a wire endpoint recalculates its shortest legal route.
- Wires attached to a moving or rotated component are rerouted as the pin moves.
- Moving a selected wire or group recalculates routes even when the existing path was translated with the endpoints.
- Routes continue to respect the drawing area and the existing wire-overlap limit.

## Verification

- Regression tests cover endpoint dragging, moving a connected component, and moving a selected wire as a whole.
